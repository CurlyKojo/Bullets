# Launch plan

## Before the first post

1. Create the account, switch it to **Creator**, fill in everything from `PROFILE.md`.
2. Upload the avatar, make the 7 highlights (post a story of each cover, save it to the
   highlight, delete the story).
3. Follow 30–50 accounts in your niches (3D printing, Raspberry Pi, space, indie dev,
   Air Force life). Instagram uses who you follow to figure out who to show you to.
4. Post the first **three** Reels on day one (001, 002, 004), then pin them. A brand new
   profile with one post converts nobody. Three gives a visitor a reason to follow.

## Schedule

One Reel a day for the first 3 weeks. That's what the 21 posts in `posts/` are for.

- **When**: 6–8 PM Pacific on weekdays, 10 AM–noon on weekends. Check Insights → Audience
  after two weeks and move to whenever your followers are actually on.
- **Order**: the queue already alternates projects so nobody sees the same thing twice in
  a row. Ghost and Flow show up most because they're the most shareable and the most
  useful.
- **Stories**: 2–3 a day, low effort. A photo of the printer, a bug you're fighting, a poll
  ("what should Wisp do next?"). Stories keep existing followers warm; Reels find new ones.

## Posting a Reel (2 minutes)

1. Open the day's download page, save the MP4 and the cover.
2. Instagram → + → Reel → pick the MP4.
3. **Add the song** from the page. Start it so its first big hit lands at the "downbeat"
   second. Every cut is timed to that tempo.
4. Cover → "Add from camera roll" → the cover JPG.
5. Paste the caption (copy button on the page). Hashtags are already in it, 5 max.
6. Share. Then stay on the app for 15 minutes and answer every comment. Early replies are
   the strongest signal you can send.

## What makes these videos perform

The renderer already does the mechanical parts. Keep these in mind when writing new posts:

- **The hook is the whole game.** Under 10 words, first frame already moving, says what's
  wild about it. "I 3D printed a Ghost that actually talks back" beats "Check out my latest
  project".
- **Numbers stop the scroll.** 32 hours, 500 g, 1 meter, $165. Every post should have one.
- **Cut on the beat, every 2 seconds or less.** The planner snaps everything to the song's
  grid. Don't fight it with long scenes.
- **Short wins.** 12–20 s Reels get watched to the end and replayed, which is the metric
  that matters most. The planner refuses anything over 34 s.
- **End with a reason to follow**: "part 2 is the wiring", "STLs are free", "what should I
  build next?" Questions in the caption get comments; comments get reach.
- **Real only.** Every fact in a post has to be true and in the project's repo. Example
  numbers in Flow demos are labelled as examples in the caption.

## Growth moves worth doing by hand

- **Comment on bigger accounts in your niches** (3D printing, Raspberry Pi, Destiny, space)
  within the first 30 minutes of their posts. Real comments, not "nice 🔥".
- **Post the Ghost to r/DestinyTheGame and r/3Dprinting** with a link to the profile. The
  Ghost is your most viral project by a mile.
- **Collab posts**: when someone prints your Ghost, ask to collab-post their make.
- **Cross-post every Reel to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.** Same file, same caption. The
  videos are already 9:16 with safe margins for all three apps.
- **"Comment GHOST and I'll DM you the files."** Works extremely well. Do it by hand at
  first; if it takes off, a tool like ManyChat automates the DM.

## Weekly check (10 minutes, Sundays)

Insights → Reels, sort by **average watch time** and **shares**. The top format gets more
posts next week. Whatever got the least watch time, figure out why the hook didn't land and
write a better one.
